# Lesson 5 — The Movie Shop and its customer portal — The catalogue

Topic for: shop owners, managers and staff. Source: IntraForge Training Guide, Lesson 5 — The Movie Shop and its customer portal.

Screenshot: `screenshots/web/41-movie-catalogue.png` — The Movie Shop catalogue

![The Movie Shop catalogue](http://localhost:8765/screenshots/web/41-movie-catalogue.png)

- **Storage** on the left (each drive, its free space) and the **Today** card (titles sold, credits, new customers).
- **Folders** across the top; click one to see its titles below. **Search titles** finds a title by name.
- Hover a poster: **Edit** (name, year, category, own price, hide, **Change poster**) and **Add** to the cart.

Screenshot: `screenshots/web/42-movie-edit-title.png` — Editing a title and its poster

![Editing a title and its poster](http://localhost:8765/screenshots/web/42-movie-edit-title.png)

- **Prices** sets one price for everything and one per category; a title can have its own.
