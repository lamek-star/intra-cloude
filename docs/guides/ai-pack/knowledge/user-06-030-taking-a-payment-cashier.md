# Lesson 6 — IntraForge Pay — Taking a payment (cashier)

Topic for: shop owners, managers and staff. Source: IntraForge Training Guide, Lesson 6 — IntraForge Pay.

Screenshot: `screenshots/web/60-pay-checkout.png` — The Pay checkout screen

![The Pay checkout screen](http://localhost:8765/screenshots/web/60-pay-checkout.png)

1. Choose your **Counter** once (the browser remembers it).
2. Type the **amount** and press **Generate QR**.
3. The customer scans the QR code with the wallet app and confirms the amount.
4. The status turns **paid** and the receipt appears under **Today's transactions**.
