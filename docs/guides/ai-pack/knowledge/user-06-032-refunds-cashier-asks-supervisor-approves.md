# Lesson 6 — IntraForge Pay — Refunds (cashier asks, supervisor approves)

Topic for: shop owners, managers and staff. Source: IntraForge Training Guide, Lesson 6 — IntraForge Pay.

In **Refunds**, type the **receipt number**, the **amount** and the **reason** and press **Request refund**. Another
person, a supervisor, must approve it before the credits go back.
