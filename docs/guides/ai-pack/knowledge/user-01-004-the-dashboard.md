# Lesson 1 — Signing in and finding your way — The dashboard

Topic for: shop owners, managers and staff. Source: IntraForge Training Guide, Lesson 1 — Signing in and finding your way.

After signing in you see the **Dashboard**: your organizations, the health of the system and the latest activity.

Screenshot: `screenshots/web/03-dashboard.png` — The dashboard

![The dashboard](http://localhost:8765/screenshots/web/03-dashboard.png)

On the left is the **sidebar**: Dashboard, Apps, Organizations, and at the bottom your name, **Log out**, **Take the
tour** and **Theme**.

- **Search (Ctrl K):** press **Ctrl** and **K** together anywhere to find a page, a bucket, a database or an app by
  typing part of its name.

Screenshot: `screenshots/web/04-command-palette.png` — Ctrl K: type to find any page

![Ctrl K: type to find any page](http://localhost:8765/screenshots/web/04-command-palette.png)

- **Theme:** the **Theme** button switches between *like this computer*, *light* and *dark*.

Screenshot: `screenshots/web/34-dashboard-dark.png` — The dashboard in dark mode

![The dashboard in dark mode](http://localhost:8765/screenshots/web/34-dashboard-dark.png)
