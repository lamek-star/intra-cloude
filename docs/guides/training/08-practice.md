# Lesson 8 — Practice and help

## A training day plan

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 30 min | Lesson 1 together: signing in, the dashboard, Ctrl K, your account |
| 45 min | Lesson 4 (or 5) for the counter staff, with the exercise on a test copy |
| 30 min | Lesson 6: a payment with the wallet app, a refund request and its approval |
| 30 min | The owner: Lessons 2 and 7 — people, roles, backups, health |
| 15 min | Questions; each person does their exercise alone |

## Checklists

**Opening the shop (owner):** the Control Center is green · the dashboard shows no health alert · last night's backup
is "Done" with "Restore tested".

**Every sale (cashier):** right customer · right quantities · payment confirmed (paid, or cash taken) · receipt or
invoice given.

**Closing (manager):** today's sales per counter in Pay · unpaid invoices checked · low-stock list read.

## When something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The page does not open | Is the shop PC on? Open the Control Center there: is IntraForge started and green? |
| "You don't have permission" | Your role does not allow this; ask the owner for the right role |
| "Too many attempts, wait a minute" | Wrong password several times: wait one minute |
| A certificate warning on another PC | Run **Trust-IntraForge-on-this-PC.cmd** once on that PC |
| Another PC cannot reach IntraForge | The shop Wi-Fi must be *Private*; Control Center > Shop network > **Update the address** |
| The licence banner says read-only | Activate the licence (Lesson 2) — your data is safe |
| A Movie Shop copy failed | Is the USB drive still plugged in and has room? Open the sale and press **Retry** |
| A payment is still "waiting" | The customer must confirm in the wallet; the QR expires after a few minutes — make a new one |
